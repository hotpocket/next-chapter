This is next-chapter, the public assembly repository, and the static site you are listening on is the artifact this chapter tours. The landing page, index dot h-t-m-l, carries the whole library inline: three books — landry-ui, repo-story, and next-chapter — each crediting the narrator Brandon Landry, together sixteen chapters and sixteen summary tracks, about four and a half hours. That three-book shape came from two prompts: a two-book kernel in the July twentieth foundation session and a trilogy addition the next day. Inside each book runs the player, the vendored landry-ui vanilla component, giving chapter navigation, resume, playback speed, time-synced transcripts with follow and reading modes, and text sizing — a reuse decided live across three foundation-session prompts. The Full and Summary toggle is the reviewer's time-budget feature, three-minute summaries against roughly twenty-minute chapters, and the most heavily documented feature in the repo, driven by a prompt chain from origin through two corrections. Supporting pieces: a transcripts file cache-keyed on its own content hash rather than modification time; a transcription-error feedback flag that is present but inert, saving only to local storage with no backend wired; an installable offline service worker with byte-range slicing and legacy eviction; the Next Chapter favicon fixed by a two-prompt exchange; and provenance documents pinning each book's source commit. The favicon and toggle carry verbatim prompts; the service worker, feedback, and cache key are vendored and stated as inference.
