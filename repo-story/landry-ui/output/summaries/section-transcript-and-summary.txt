Chapter two covers the player's reading surface — the transcript panel and the features that act on the text while the audio plays. The time-synced transcript highlights the chunk being spoken and lets any chunk be clicked to seek there. Follow mode, on by default, auto-scrolls to keep the active chunk about a third of the way down the pane; a manual scroll gesture switches it off, and explicit navigation switches it back on. Reading mode strips the player to the transcript alone, full-bleed edge to edge, with a mini play button in the header. The text-size stepper scales the type geometrically, one point two five per step, from about sixty-four percent of the base to nearly double, persisting the exponent. Scene-break auto-pause holds two seconds at the silent asterisk dividers, then resumes on its own.

The centerpiece is the Full-Summary toggle: for books carrying condensed summary tracks, switching modes swaps the audio source, the transcript chunks, and the entire time model at once — a second clock built on summary durations.

Provenance is split. Transcript, follow, reading mode, text-size, and scene-break predate the prompt exports and rest on commit and recap paraphrase. The Full-Summary toggle, from the July twenty-first session, carries verbatim prompts tracing a full correction arc: the initial request, a placement fix, and the time-model fix that created the summary clock.
