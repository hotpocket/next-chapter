Chapter two covers the player's reading surface: the transcript panel and everything that acts on the text while the audio runs.

The time-synced transcript highlights the chunk matching the audio's current position, walking the highlight down the page in step with the voice; clicking any chunk seeks the audio to it. Follow mode, on by default, auto-scrolls to keep the active chunk parked about a third of the way down the pane — a manual scroll gesture turns it off, and explicit navigation turns it back on. Reading mode strips the player down to the transcript alone, full-bleed edge to edge, with a mini play button in the header. The text-size stepper scales the type geometrically, each step one-point-two-five times the last, from about sixty-four percent of the base to nearly double, persisting the exponent. Scene-break auto-pause holds two seconds at the unspoken asterisk dividers, then resumes on its own.

The chapter's centerpiece is the Full-Summary toggle, the best-documented feature in the player. It appears only for books that carry a summary track, and switching to Summary swaps the audio source, the transcript chunks, and the entire time model at once — a second clock built on condensed durations.

Prompts drove this chapter unevenly. Transcript, follow, reading mode, text-size, and scene-break rest on commit and recap paraphrase, since they predate the prompt exports. The Full-Summary toggle, built July twenty-first, has verbatim prompts showing a correction arc: the initial request, a placement fix, and the time-model fix creating the summary clock.
