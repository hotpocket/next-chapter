Meeting the player, in brief.

landry-ui is a component library in plain JavaScript with no build step, and its audiobook player is the component playing this narration. This chapter covers the player's core; the book runs four chapters — the core, the reading surface, offline, then integration and tests.

The features, by name. The library view lists each book with a chapter-count and duration line, a progress dot colored by how far along you are, and a configurable heading. The resume system saves your position to the browser's local storage every five seconds and on exit, and auto-reopens your last book. The per-chapter audio model is the pivotal decision: one small M4A file per chapter, one loaded at a time, a chapter-local clock underneath and a book-wide clock plus a binary search on top. The chapter panel shows per-row progress and collapses to short Ch labels when squeezed narrow. The transport gives thirty-second skips, chapter steps, play and pause, and a speed button cycling from zero point seven five up to two. The track bar drags with pointer capture, follows live within the loaded chapter, and loads on release into another. Hash routing makes each book a shareable address.

On prompts: everything here predates the project's prompt exports, so provenance is commit-message and session-note paraphrase, and the reason for the per-chapter model is inference from a later recap.
